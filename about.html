About TeaPearl

Five seats.One tea room.

TeaPearl rolls loose tea into pearls and packs each tin to order. These five places run the room. The names go here when you are ready to fill them.

The five seats

  • CEO

    Chief Executive Officer

    Sets the direction of the tea room and what the shop stands for.

  • CFO

    Chief Financial Officer

    Keeps the prices, the tins, and the books in one clear line.

  • CTP

    Chief of Tea Production

    Watches the leaf, the roll, and the pearl that goes in the tin.

  • COO

    Chief Operating Officer

    Packs the order and gets the sealed tin to the city on the request.

  • CMO

    Chief Marketing Officer

    Tells the story of the pearl, from the counter to the cup.
